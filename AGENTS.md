# AI Judge — Bharat Bench Agent Protocols & System Architecture

## Project Overview
**AI Judge (Bharat Bench)** is an autonomous multimodal digital courtroom and dispute resolution tribunal built for the **Meta Open Source Hackathon 2026**.
It integrates sovereign Indian procedural statutes with Meta open-weights AI architectures:
- **Civil Procedure**: Order XX Rule 6 (Judicial Decrees), Order XXXVII (Summary Suits), Order XXI (Execution).
- **Substantive Law**: Indian Contract Act, 1872 (Section 74 Liquidated Damages Mitigation), IT Act, 2000 (Section 43A).
- **Evidentiary Law**: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (Section 63 Admissibility of Electronic Records).
- **Criminal Procedure**: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (Section 480 Bail, Section 173 Chargesheet).

## Core AI & Multi-Agent Model Matrix
- **Meta Muse Spark Open 1026 (via Baseten)**: Autonomous adversarial litigation stream, real-time interlocutory objections, and judicial reasoning.
- **Meta Muse Wonder 1026 (via Modal Labs)**: Deterministic judicial decree synthesis, statutory interest calculation, and enforceable seal generation.
- **Meta SAM 3.1**: Sub-pixel forensic document inspection and forgery detection for certified legal exhibits.
